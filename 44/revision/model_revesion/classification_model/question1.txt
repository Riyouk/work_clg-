Scenario: Customer Churn Classification — TeleConnect Ltd

You are hired as a Machine Learning Engineer at TeleConnect Ltd, a telecom company that wants to reduce customer churn. The company has collected user account, usage, support, and billing data from multiple sources. Your objective is to build a robust classification system that predicts whether a customer will churn within the next 30 days.

14. Choosing baseline model:

15. Model selection:

20. Evaluation metrics:

22. Feature importance analysis:

23. Model comparison and selection:
